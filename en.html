🌿 Leafdex Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Sodam Sky Labs ("we") makes and provides the plant care app Leafdex (잎새도감, "the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles and where it is stored.

1. Personal information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information.

  • There is no sign-up or login.
  • The app has no server and does not send anything you enter to us or anyone else.
  • The app does not use ads, analytics tools or location data.

2. Information stored only on your device

What you enter while using the app is stored only on your device (in the app's private storage). We cannot see it.

  • Plant names, watering and fertilizing intervals, reminder times, growing conditions and notes
  • Watering and fertilizing history, plant status entries
  • Plant photos you take or pick (kept as copies in the app's folder)

Deleting the app removes this information from your device.

If device backup is turned on, your phone's operating system may include the app's data in a backup to your own account (Android: Google account backup, iPhone: iCloud Backup). That backup is handled by your phone's operating system and we cannot see it. Reinstalling the app with the same account may bring your records back. You can turn device backup off in your phone's settings.

3. Permissions the app uses

PermissionUsed for
CameraOnly when you take a plant photo. Photos stay on your device.
Photo pickerChoosing a plant photo from your gallery with the system photo picker. Only the photos you pick are passed to the app; it does not read your whole gallery.
Photos / storage (save)Saving plant card images to your gallery. On Android 12 and below the app may ask for storage write permission.
NotificationsReminders for watering and fertilizing days. Reminders are scheduled on your device and never go through an outside server.

You can turn permissions off at any time in your phone's Settings › Apps › Leafdex › Permissions. Everything else keeps working without them.

4. Information you export yourself

  • Backup file: tapping "Create backup" makes a file with your plants, history, photos and app settings (season start months, rain spots, trips and the like) and hands it to the app you choose (for example, Google Drive or a messenger).
  • Sharing a plant card: tapping "Share" hands the card image to the app you choose.
  • Log spreadsheet (CSV): tapping "Export as spreadsheet" hands a file with your care and status logs to the app you choose.
  • Shop and map search: tapping a shop button in the Plant mart opens a shopping service (Naver Shopping, Coupang) with the plant's name as the search term, and the nearby nurseries button searches a map service for garden centres. Only the search term is passed on, and the service that opens follows its own privacy policy.

Files you export follow the privacy policy of the app or service you chose. We never receive or see them.

5. Sharing with third parties and processors

Because we collect no personal information, we do not share it with or entrust it to anyone.

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no one's personal information, including that of children under 14 (or the age set by your local law).

7. Changes to this policy

If a new feature changes what information the app handles (for example, sharing plants with other people), we will update this policy first and post the changes and the new effective date on this page.
